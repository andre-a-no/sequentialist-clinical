# CDISC pilot study, ADaM (cdiscpilot01)

`adsl.json` and `adtte.json` are the subject-level dataset and the
time-to-event dataset (time to the first dermatologic event) of the CDISC
pilot study, in Dataset-JSON 1.1, as published by CDISC in
https://github.com/cdisc-org/DataExchange-DatasetJson (`examples/adam`),
under the MIT license in `LICENSE` (Copyright (c) 2022 cdisc). They are
used unchanged, by the tests only, and are not part of the package.
